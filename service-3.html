FAQ

Questions we hear often

Do you work with startups or only established companies?

Both. We scope engagements to fit — a focused MVP sprint for early-stage teams, or a longer roadmap for established products.

How is a project priced?

Fixed-scope projects are quoted after discovery; ongoing work runs on a monthly retainer. You'll always get a written estimate before work starts.

Can you work with our existing codebase?

Yes — we regularly join projects mid-flight. A short technical audit up front tells us (and you) what shape the codebase is in before we commit to a timeline.

What happens after launch?

We offer post-launch support and maintenance retainers, but you always own the code and infrastructure — no lock-in.
